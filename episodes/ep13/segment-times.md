GLOSSARY
• Levels of AGI (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.02462): Google DeepMind's 2023 framework — Level 1 Emerging (≈ unskilled human; ChatGPT), Level 2 Competent (50th percentile of skilled adults), Level 3 Expert (90th), Level 4 Exceptional (99th), Level 5 Superhuman. Graded per task, and "general" means broad, not narrow: AlphaFold folds proteins, Fable writes code.
• Chris's grade: coding is already Level 4 — "I don't know a single person who is better." Jackson's test is blunter: "$100K → $1M, and it works." Not yet.
• Augmentative vs autonomous: does the system make you a super-skilled knowledge worker, or operate on its own? Most of what we use is still the former.
• MCP postmortem: Model Context Protocol servers — the tool-plumbing standard of 2025. Verdict on tape: "a huge, huge waste of time." Skills (reusable instruction packs) are what stuck.
• Sycophancy / AI psychosis: models that tell you you're a genius, and users who lose contact with tangible reality because of it. Chris's antidote: a good internal mechanism for being wrong.
• The novice/expert study: Brynjolfsson's call-center research — AI lifted low-performers ~34%, top performers ~0%. We're skeptical it holds for engineering.

• Euro stablecoin: ~€300M in issuance vs hundreds of billions in dollar stablecoins. Maybe an FX play; the Euro is not the Eurodollar. Verdict: flushed — "we'll dig around in the sewers if we ever need it again."
• The chicken egg app (coopr — https://jacksoncook.github.io/coopr/): Jackson's "make me a million dollars" prompt → market research (12M US households with chickens) → an iPhone app for counting eggs. 11 downloads (incl. girlfriend), $0.
