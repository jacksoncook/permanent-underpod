### Z — MARQUEE: the AI audit of Saez–Zucman (Jackson, ≤9 min)

**Start:** "On Tuesday, an anonymous reader sent Tyler Cowen an AI audit of 138 works by the two most influential inequality economists alive. Cowen's response: we need a radical revaluation of this work. Wednesday, the economists called it grotesque, and Jason Furman audited the audit with another AI."

- **The audit.** Site "Built 28 October 2026." Two blind auditors, Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, each "recomputed the headline numbers from the authors' own files and, where possible, from the original sources"; a third unnamed adjudicator "tried to refute each one, discarded those that did not survive… graded what remained by how far it undermines the paper's own headline claim." Not published: who ran it, the prompts, the code (scripts are cited but not released), how many flags were discarded, auditor agreement, any false-positive rate. [MR post](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/10/errors-in-the-research-of-emmanuel-saez-and-gabriel-zucman.html) · [Audit site](https://saez-zucman-audit-replication.vercel.app/)
- **The numbers, as they actually break down (our tally of all 138 pages matches the site).** 2,035 problems = **147 major, 713 moderate, 1,175 minor**. "69%" = 95 of 138 works with at least one *major* problem (136 of 138 have one of any severity). "97%" = of major+moderate flags with a clear direction, 703 overstate the paper's claim vs 19 understate it; 138 directional-unclear flags are excluded. Only **11** of 2,035 are tagged "replication failure." About half the majors are tagged **"previously known"** (75 of 146 parsed); only 85 of 146 are "confirmed," the rest "likely" or "plausible." "138 papers" includes *The Triumph of Injustice*, the Warren and Sanders scoring letters, a VoxEU column, Prop 40 expert memos, unpublished replies to Summers–Sarin and Auten–Splinter, and Saez's Clark Medal tribute to Zucman; roughly half are journal articles, many co-authored with Chetty, Piketty, Kleven.
- **A "major" that's real:** the 2019 claim that the Forbes 400 paid a lower total tax rate (23%) than the bottom half (24.2%). The audit: the gap is ~1.2 points; netting refundable credits flips it from −1.17 to +2.00; the authors' own later data vintages put the top 400 at 27–29% vs 25–26%. **A "major" that's small:** the Warren scoring flag is graded major while its own entry says "the effect on the main estimates is only a few percent." [Triumph page](https://saez-zucman-audit-replication.vercel.app/p/saez-zucman-2019-triumph-of-injustice.html) · [Warren page](https://saez-zucman-audit-replication.vercel.app/p/saez-zucman-2019-warren-wealth-tax-scoring.html)
- **The reactions, Oct 29.** Saez & Zucman (joint, posted by Zucman): "no merit… a grotesque use of artificial intelligence"; "if you prompt an AI to find 'errors' in a paper, it will oblige"; "merchants of doubt"; ties the timing to Prop 40. Furman: "the critic's errors all go in one direction," "most of the 'errors' are disputes over methods, assumptions or wording," the audit "counted the same problem over and over," and his own check used "a short prompt"; also "Piketty-Saez-Zucman work has many flaws that all go one direction. Spreading slop hurts all three causes." Phil Magness: the AI confirms a decade of human replications. Karl Whelan: "this is not the way to do this… Anonymous. No code." Top MR comment (180 votes): "This clearly needs a control." Cowen's follow-up post: the 97% is "the most damning observation," and MR's archive gets audited next ("Alex has prepared this"). HN: 2 points. [Zucman thread](https://x.com/gabriel_zucman/status/2115853565730205847) · [Furman](https://x.com/jasonfurman/status/2115639571270812064) · [Furman, Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/jasonfurman.bsky.social/post/3myzpuq57vt2t) · [Magness](https://x.com/PhilWMagness/status/2115545335103734152) · [Whelan](https://bsky.app/profile/karlwhelan.bsky.social/post/3myxvhlsyo22b) · [Cowen follow-up](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/10/ai-dominates-human-peer-reviews.html)
- **Why the 97% may be built in (Furman's point, in mechanism terms).** Severity is graded by how far an error undermines the paper's headline claim. An error that *understates* the claim can't undermine it, so it lands in "minor" and drops out of the directional count. Some entries say the fix "would probably strengthen the paper's point." The fair test, which nobody has run: the same pipeline on Auten–Splinter or on Cowen. [Example entry](https://saez-zucman-audit-replication.vercel.app/p/ring-seim-zucman-2026-personal-holding-companies.html)

Cowen's own trail: Litvak's planted-error test caught 71/100 single, 93 pooled, "I didn't measure false positives"; Toda (June): "no model located a true error without substantial human guidance"; King & Worthington (Oct, Opus 4.8): AI reviews beat top human reviews.
