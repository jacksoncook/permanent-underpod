### M — MARQUEE: the Ledger implant (Chris, ≤9 min)

**Start:** "Oct 9, Ledger on X: if you bought from this reseller in the last 90 days, don't set up your device. By then it was over. 324 wallets, 47 minutes."

- **What it is.** A physical implant inside Nano X enclosures, installed before sale, sold through authorized Southeast Asian reseller **CryptoBilis** (Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines). Ledger Donjon, Oct 13: "The implant sits inside the enclosure and reads the data shown on the screen... transmits them over LTE using a provisioned eSIM." It taps the OLED's SPI bus, pattern-matches glyphs to capture the 24 words during setup, and exfiltrates over cellular. The Secure Element, OS, signing, infrastructure, and direct sales channel are untouched, so the genuine check passes. [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/13/ledger-says-spy-chip-inside-cryptobilis-ledger-devices-transmitted-wallet-keys-over-mobile-network) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/86-million-crypto-heist-linked-to-ledger-signer-hardware-implant/)

Oct 11 (72-hour update): "the volume of impacted devices is limited," all confirmed cases via CryptoBilis. Oct 13: Donjon mechanism disclosure. Oct 14: Ledger asks resellers across SEA and Hong Kong to halt sales. **No loss total, victim count, serial range, or ledger.com blog post from Ledger as of Oct 15**; all comms are X posts. [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-10-09-ledger-cryptobilis-fund-losses-418163) · [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/ledger-says-all-confirmed-cases-trace-to-reseller-cryptobilis-gives-no-loss-total/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ledger-asks-sellers-across-asia-to-halt-sales-after-supply-chain-attack/)
