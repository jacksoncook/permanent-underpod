### L — Layer-2 series, entry three: Liquid, explained (Tyler, ≤7 min)

**Start:** "Liquid is the oldest bitcoin layer two still running, it's the only one Tether issues on, and in September it minted 4,000 bitcoin from nothing. Here's how it works, and then what happened when it didn't."

Record the explainer block (first ~3 min) as one clean, self-contained run with no references to earlier in the episode, so it can be lifted as a standalone.

**The explainer (Tyler, ≤3 min):**

- **What it is.** A sidechain: a separate blockchain with its own blocks, running next to Bitcoin, where the coin is L-BTC, meant to be backed one-to-one by real bitcoin. Live since September 2018. The software is Elements, a fork of Bitcoin Core. [How Liquid works](https://docs.liquid.net/docs/how-liquid-works)
- **Who runs it.** No miners. Fifteen "functionaries" (machines run by member companies) take turns proposing blocks, and 11 of the 15 must sign each one. A block a minute, settled after two. The wider Federation is 80+ member companies; only 15 sign. [Technical overview](https://docs.liquid.net/docs/technical-overview)
- **Getting in (peg-in).** Send bitcoin to an address the federation controls, wait 102 Bitcoin confirmations (about 17 hours), claim the same amount of L-BTC. [Peg-in / peg-out](https://docs.liquid.net/docs/advanced-pegin-pegout)
- **Getting out (peg-out).** Burn L-BTC, and the federation releases bitcoin. Only holders of a Peg-out Authorization Key can ask, so a normal user goes through a member service or swaps on an exchange. This is the series test: **there is no unilateral exit.** You can't leave without the federation's signatures. The only fallback is three emergency keys behind a timelock, for a federation that has stopped entirely.
- **What you get for the trust.** Speed (two minutes to final). Confidential Transactions: amounts and asset types are hidden by default, though who paid whom is still visible. Issued assets: anyone can mint a token, which is why USDT lives here. Fees are paid in L-BTC.
- **One line to land it:** Lightning and Ark give you a transaction you can broadcast yourself. Liquid gives you fifteen signers and a promise.

**The live stakes (≤4 min):**

- **Recap, 30 seconds.** Sept 6: an Elements cache bug minted ~4,000 unbacked L-BTC, which left through a member's peg-out key. The attacker returned 3,400 and kept ~602. Reserve 4,205 → 197 BTC. [Blockstream assessment](https://blog.blockstream.com/liquid-network-security-incident-assessment/)
