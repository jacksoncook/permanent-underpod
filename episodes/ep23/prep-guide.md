- **Premise check.** Not an outage: a breach, then a containment shutdown Chainalysis chose. Oct 30 is the disclosure date; the intrusion was "this month" (October), date not given. - **The timeline, in Chainalysis's words.**
  - Oct (undated): "an external actor using offensive agentic techniques to gain unauthorized access to Chainalysis systems and internal tools."
  - Oct 30: discloses; says user IDs, names, emails and role labels were accessed, and "no evidence of unauthorized access to investigation content, authentication credentials, or session tokens." "This security incident is not related to the recent crypto theft at Binance."
  - Nov 2: Reactor, Data Solutions and Rapid go offline. Also Nov 2, per the later update: "we discovered certain customer information including names, usernames, password hashes and live tokens for all Chainalysis users were exposed to a malicious actor." Not disclosed until the night of Nov 4.
  - Nov 4, morning: an "on-path attack" exposed "a subset of customer passwords"; "an ongoing multifront cyberattack." 15:10 UTC: "Due to abnormal activity, we have taken KYT (the user interface and API) offline alongside other address screening products and the login page." Night: the all-users disclosure, plus "customers whose KYT accounts appear to have been accessed without authorization" get notified.
  - Nov 6: phased KYT / Address Screening restore, behind an IP allowlist, password reset and API key rotation. Nov 7, 19:31 UTC: Reactor, KYT and Address Screening "back online." Nov 10: Data Solutions back. Nov 12: incident still open (Rapid and Academy down).
- **The walk-back is the story inside the story.** Oct 30: no credentials or session tokens. Nov 4: live tokens for every user, known since Nov 2. Chainalysis still says "no evidence that any investigations data has been accessed or Chainalysis attribution data has been manipulated." [The Record](https://therecord.media/chainalysis-all-users-hit-by-agentic-cyberattack) · [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/chainalysis-discloses-apparent-breach-of-customers-accounts)

Its fix includes "using sophisticated large language models to support a broader review of our platform's security." Microsoft's Nov 5 post on ARTEX (an agent framework that harvests secrets from public GitHub, one case went GitHub to Azure Key Vault in five minutes) does not name Chainalysis. - **Who stopped.**
