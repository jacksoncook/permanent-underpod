# Perp of Fortune intros

Five options to reuse. Tyler jokes assume he still hasn't contributed capital.

## 1. Jackson's original

“This is the Perp of Fortune, where Chris and I stake our money for fortune or destitution on a perpetual future, one of the highest-volatility ‘investing’ instruments around, while Tyler criticizes our leverage choice. Over to Chris.”

## 2. Recommended

“Welcome to Perp of Fortune, where Chris and I put real money into leveraged perpetual futures in pursuit of fortune or destitution, while Tyler tells us we picked the wrong leverage. Chris, what's the damage?”

## 3. Punchier

“This is Perp of Fortune. Chris and I supply the money. Leverage supplies the consequences. Tyler supplies unsolicited feedback. Chris, what are we buying?”

## 4. Closest to the original

“This is Perp of Fortune, where Chris and I stake our money on a leveraged perpetual future. Fortune or destitution awaits. Tyler risks nothing but has strong opinions about our leverage. Over to Chris.”

## 5. More ridiculous

“Welcome to Perp of Fortune, where Chris and I pursue financial independence through instruments designed to separate us from our finances. Tyler supervises without contributing capital. Chris, take it away.”
